FAQ

Simply Endorsed CFI questions

Is Simply Endorsed CFI the same as the Simply Endorsed app?
Yes. This page targets the phrase people search, while the actual searchable FAA endorsement app remains at /simply-endorsed/.
Can a CFI use Simply Endorsed instead of AC 61-65K?
No. Simply Endorsed is a reference aid. CFIs and ground instructors should verify endorsement applicability against the current FAA guidance, the pilot's certificate path, the training record, and current regulations before signing.
Does Simply Endorsed include CFI checkride endorsements?
Yes. The tool includes the flight instructor endorsement entries from AC 61-65K, including knowledge-test and practical-test endorsement context. See the CFI initial checkride endorsement guide for a plain-English overview.
