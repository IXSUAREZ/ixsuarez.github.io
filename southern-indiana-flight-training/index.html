Southern Indiana flight training

Flight training for Southern Indiana students, minutes across the river

Live in New Albany, Jeffersonville, Clarksville, Sellersburg, or Floyds Knobs? Bowman Field (KLOU) in Louisville is roughly 15–25 minutes across the river — a towered, student-friendly airport where I teach one-on-one as an FAA Certificated Flight Instructor.

The Kentuckiana advantage

Bowman Field is closer than you think

A lot of Southern Indiana students assume they need an Indiana airport to train in Indiana. In practice, KLOU is a short drive over the bridge from New Albany or Jeffersonville — often faster than reaching a farther Indiana field — and it gives you a control tower, well-maintained runways, and quick access to quiet practice areas. Learning to work with ATC from your first lesson is a genuine advantage you'll carry to every airport afterward.

I'm also familiar with Clark Regional Airport (KJVY) in Sellersburg, so if you fly out of there or want cross-country work that includes it, that's already in the picture.

What Indiana students train for here

  • Private pilot training from zero experience through checkride
  • Discovery flights — the low-commitment first step
  • Ground school, in person or fully remote
  • Flight reviews and rust-removal for certificated pilots
  • Written-test prep and checkride oral prep
Remote-first option

Start the ground work from Indiana — no bridge required

Most of the knowledge side of training — airspace, weather, regulations, performance, navigation, written-test prep — works well over a video call. Indiana students often knock out ground sessions remotely during the week and come across the river for flight lessons on the weekend. That cuts drive time, keeps aircraft hours productive, and lets you build momentum before you ever commit to a regular schedule.

Questions from Indiana students

Southern Indiana flight training FAQs

Do you teach students from Indiana?
Yes — regularly. Students come from New Albany, Jeffersonville, Clarksville, Sellersburg, Floyds Knobs, and across Kentuckiana. There's no residency requirement for flight training; the only thing that matters is getting to Bowman Field for the flight lessons, and most ground work can happen remotely.
How far is Bowman Field from New Albany or Jeffersonville?
Roughly 15–25 minutes by car depending on where you start and bridge traffic — comparable to or faster than reaching many Indiana airports from the same towns. Bowman Field sits on the east side of Louisville just off US-60, a straight shot over the river.
Can I do ground school remotely from Indiana?
Yes. Airspace, weather, regulations, performance, navigation, and written-test prep all work well over video call, and remote sessions are easy to schedule around work. Many Indiana students do ground sessions remotely during the week and flight lessons at KLOU on weekends. Details are on the remote ground instruction page.
Can Indiana residents book a discovery flight?
Absolutely — a discovery flight is open to anyone, and it's the standard first step before committing to training. You'll fly out of Bowman Field, take the controls, and see both sides of the river from the air. Details are on the discovery flight page.
