Oral-prep FAQs

When should I start oral prep?
Start before the final week. Oral prep works best when you have time to find weak areas, fix explanations, and bring questions back to your instructor.
Can you sign me off for the checkride?
Yes, for airplane single-engine. As your CFI, I can give the checkride recommendation and required endorsements once you are ready. If you are training with another instructor, this session is ground-prep support — ACS organization and scenario practice — and the recommendation still comes from them.
What should I bring?
Bring your ACS, written-test report, syllabus, aircraft checklist/POH notes, and a list of areas you do not feel ready to explain out loud.
What does this cost?
Oral prep is ground instruction, billed at the same hourly rate: $45 per hour. Most sessions run one to two hours, and you'll get an exact quote before we start — no surprises.
