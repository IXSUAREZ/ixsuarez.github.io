About this reference

This is the hosted exterior model selected by Rotax’s configurator, published in 2021. This view selects gearbox type 2 and the 0.9 kW starter. Equipment and later engine revisions may differ.

Use the animated engine for individual part selection, moving internals and assembly controls. This exterior reference does not expose every individual part or the sealed electronics inside the CDI modules.

The hosted model is available to view, but is not offered as downloadable CAD. Its geometry is not included in the teaching model. Model and configurator credit: Rotax / Frontline; hosted by Sketchfab.
