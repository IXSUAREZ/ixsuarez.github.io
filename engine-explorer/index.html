Crank & Core Engine Explorer

About these engine study models

Crank & Core lets you explore the parts and systems of the Rotax 912 ULS and the Lycoming IO-360-L2A used in the Cessna 172S. These are educational reconstructions, not complete OEM replicas or maintenance instructions.

Start with aircraft systems guides, then select an engine to examine its parts. Use the in-app Resources panel for model-specific references and limitations.

For the underlying teaching concepts, see the FAA Pilot’s Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge, Aircraft Systems chapter.
