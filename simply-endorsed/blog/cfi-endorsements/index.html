CFI Endorsement Lookup Guide

Diego Suarez
· Updated May 13, 2026 · FAA reference

Pilots and instructors often search for CFI endorsements, FAA CFI endorsements, or a CFI endorsement lookup when they are trying to confirm the core initial instructor signoff path. This guide is a quick index to the AC 61-65K entries that most often appear together.

In Simply Endorsed CFI, the common CFI initial cluster centers on A.45, A.46, A.47, and A.49, with A.1 and A.2 frequently reviewed as supporting practical-test endorsements. Use this page as a starting point, read the broader FAA endorsements list when you need the full AC 61-65K context, then open the Simply Endorsed lookup tool to search by ID, FAR citation, alias, or checkride workflow before any signoff.

Core CFI endorsement cluster

A.45

Fundamentals of instructing knowledge test

Supports FOI knowledge-test readiness in the CFI initial path.

A.46

Flight instructor aeronautical knowledge test

Documents preparation for the applicable instructor-level aeronautical knowledge test.

A.47

Flight instructor practical-test readiness

Covers required ground and flight training and recommendation for the CFI practical test.

A.49

Spin training

Addresses instructional proficiency in stall awareness, spin entry, spins, and spin recovery where required.

A.1 / A.2

General practical-test support

Often reviewed alongside the CFI-specific recommendations for practical-test timing and deficiency review context.
