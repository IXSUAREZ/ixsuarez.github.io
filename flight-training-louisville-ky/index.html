Common planning questions

Flight training planning FAQs

Is this a flight school?
Not in the traditional sense — it is a planning page. Diego Suarez is an FAA Certificated Flight Instructor (airplane single-engine) and Advanced Ground Instructor based around Bowman Field, and he can take you through ground instruction, flight training, endorsements, and checkride recommendations himself, or help you make a better first decision if you plan to train elsewhere.
What should I do before booking a first lesson?
Decide your likely certificate path, understand the medical requirement, start a written-test plan, and ask how often you can realistically train. Those four items prevent a lot of expensive drift later.
Can ground instruction reduce the total cost?
It can. Ground instruction does not replace aircraft training, but it can make aircraft lessons more productive by handling concepts, procedures, regulations, and planning before you are paying for the airplane. Pairing it with flight instruction from the same instructor removes some of the back-and-forth of switching between people.
Do you help students training at another Louisville school?
Yes. Ground instruction and study planning can support students training elsewhere. Bring your syllabus, ACS weak areas, practice-test report, or questions from recent lessons.
