Personal minimums

FAA supplies a framework, not universal certificate-tier numeric recommendations. Keep these values blank until you set limits tailored to recent experience, proficiency, the aircraft, and the operation; review them with a CFI.

Student solo: these are instructor limits.

Under § 61.89, 3 SM by day and 5 SM at night are legal visibility floors, and the flight must remain within logbook limitations. Those are constraints—not recommended personal minimums.

Regulatory constraints

Check the current regulation, endorsements, aircraft limits, and instructor/operator restrictions for the actual flight.

Planning guidance

Set conservative values away from the pressure of a specific flight. Do not lower them to make today’s plan fit.
