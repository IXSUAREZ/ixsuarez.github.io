How this matrix works

The assessment combines each hazard's likelihood and severity using the FAA risk matrix. The highest unresolved result becomes the overall result. Low-risk answers never subtract from a High or Stop hazard.

  • Low: Continue full planning and recheck changing conditions.
  • Moderate: Mitigate where practical and recheck before departure.
  • High: Do not depart on the current assessment; change the plan and rescore.
  • Stop: The current plan is a no-go until the underlying condition is removed.
