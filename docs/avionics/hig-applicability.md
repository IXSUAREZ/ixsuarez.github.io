# Apple Human Interface Guidelines applicability

**Updated:** 2026-09-22  ·  **Scope:** responsive SUAREZ.CFI website redesign  ·  **Canonical inventory:** `hig-applicability.json`

Apple HIG describes Apple platforms and native UI. This inventory translates applicable usability principles to web interactions; it does not claim Apple HIG or WCAG conformance. The 15 category overview records and 158 topic records preserve full assigned research coverage. Each record has an official source, mapped principle, intentional adaptation or N/A, planned verification ID, and scoped implementation evidence links. Evidence links are traceability to a related design-system/app artifact, not proof that every topic has been tested or passed.

## Approved website decisions

- Modern Garmin touch is the default visual language across the website and tools.
- Smoked glass is limited to the floating dock and menus; article, form, and result surfaces stay matte.
- Persistent site destinations and contextual app actions have distinct semantics. Desktop dock: Home, Training, Learn, Blog, Tools, Fly with Diego, Menu. About is available in Menu only. Mobile dock: Home, Learn, Tools, Menu.
- Tool-specific steps and actions remain in their app context. A bottom contextual control panel may group app actions; it must not be presented as the global destination navigation.
- Preserve routes, copy, calculations, data, exports, and category/risk semantics.
- Preserve the homepage animated sky JS/CSS/assets/geometry and existing reduced-motion behavior. No dock magnification.
- Aero Lab remains desktop-only by explicit product decision; native platform-specific HIG details are not literal requirements for this web experience.
