Remote ground FAQs

What works well remotely?
Written-test prep, ACS oral practice, weather, airspace, systems, regulations, performance planning, and scenario discussion all work well remotely.
What does not work well remotely?
Aircraft-specific flight training, signoffs that require direct observation, and airplane handling cannot be replaced by remote ground instruction — that part happens in person, in the airplane, with Diego at Bowman Field or with your own instructor if you train elsewhere.
Can remote help still be Louisville-specific?
Yes. If you train around Bowman Field or another Louisville-area airport, the session can use local airspace, weather, and airport context while staying focused on ground instruction.
What does this cost?
Remote ground instruction is billed at the same hourly rate as in-person instruction: $45 per hour. Most sessions run one to two hours, and you'll get an exact quote before we start — no surprises.
